A BASTIDE SINCE 1256

Arcades, carrerots and views over the countryside

Monflanquin keeps the classic structure of a South-West bastide: a central square framed by arcades, intersecting streets and very narrow passages known as carrerots. It is best explored on foot, noticing the façades and glimpses out towards the landscape.

Make time for the Bastides Museum to understand how these medieval “new towns” were planned and built.
