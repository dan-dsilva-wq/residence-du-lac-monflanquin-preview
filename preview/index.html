A SLOWER STAY IN LOT-ET-GARONNE

An easy base between the lake and the bastide

Résidence du Lac Monflanquin brings the essentials together for an uncomplicated holiday: a comfortable home for four, a terrace facing the greenery, Lac Coulon close by and Monflanquin for food, wandering and days out.

Think of the home as a quiet base: take mornings slowly, explore the countryside, then come back and settle in.

WHY STAY HERE

Everything you need to slow down

A simple, welcoming address for couples, small families and relaxed breaks with the people you know.

An independent base

Days spent outside
