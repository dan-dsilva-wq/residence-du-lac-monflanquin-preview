PRACTICAL INFORMATION

Everything you need to know

Find answers to the most common questions about your stay at Résidence du Lac Monflanquin, so you can book with confidence.

How many people can stay?

The current layout is intended for a maximum of four guests. Always confirm the exact number of people when booking.

How are the beds arranged?

The home has one bedroom with a double bed and an additional sleeping space in the living room, following the current listing layout.

What is included in the home?

The amenities currently shown include a kitchenette, Wi-Fi, bed linen and towels, a dining space and a private terrace.

What can I prepare in the kitchenette?

The kitchenette is suited to breakfasts, picnics and simple meals. Use the current Airbnb photographs and inventory for any specific equipment.

Is Wi-Fi available?

Wi-Fi is included in the current amenities. If you have a particular work or technical requirement, confirm it with the host before booking.

When is the shared pool open?

The residence’s outdoor pool opens seasonally. Dates and hours can change, so confirm them on Airbnb or with the host before you travel.

Can I fish at Lac Coulon?

Yes, during the permitted season and with a valid fishing card. Check the official local rules and charges before fishing.

Is the stay suitable for children?

The home suits small families and the residence has a playground. Children remain under adult supervision, especially beside the water and pool.

Is parking available?

On-site parking is included in the current amenities. Follow the access instructions provided after booking.

Are pets allowed?

Pet rules can change. Always use the house-rules section of the current Airbnb listing as the source of truth when booking.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Final times and arrival instructions appear in the Airbnb confirmation. Contact the host in advance if you have a particular constraint.

Is the home accessible?

Photographs alone cannot confirm particular access requirements. Ask the host before booking if steps, movement around the home or bathroom access matter to your stay.

When will I receive the exact address?

The full address and access instructions are supplied through the Airbnb booking journey and host messages.

How do booking and prices work?

Current availability, total price, fees, cancellation terms and house rules are those displayed on Airbnb at the time you book.
